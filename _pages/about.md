I am a PhD candidate at **UNIST** in the High-Assurance Mobility and Control Lab (HMCL), advised by
Professor Kwon. My research focuses on building robust perception and autonomy systems that operate
reliably in real-world conditions — validated through high-stakes environments like autonomous racing
competitions. I am the founder of **UNICORN Racing**, our lab's autonomous racing team (est. 2025).

My research interests include SLAM, LiDAR-Inertial Odometry, autonomous racing, end-to-end autonomous
driving, reinforcement learning, and robot perception.
